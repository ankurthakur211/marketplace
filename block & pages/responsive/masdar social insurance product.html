About Masdar Social Insurance Information

 

  • Consuming Masder and SPL Services is a product provided by Neotek (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider) this product provides comprehensive employment data for persons and establishments.
  • The purpose of this project is to allow ARG subsidiaries (referred as consumers) to offer the consuming Masder and SPL services service to their customers through subsidiary digital channels.
  • The end customers of the service are individuals and corporate (referred to as customers) who retrieve social insurance information.
