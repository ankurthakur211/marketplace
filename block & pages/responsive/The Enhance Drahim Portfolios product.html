About The Enhance Drahim Portfolios

 

  • The Enhance Drahim Portfolios product is designed to allow ARG subsidiaries to retrieve investment account (portfolio) information associated with a specific user, identified by their national ID or Iqama number. This service enables secure, OAuth2-protected access to customer investment data via digital channels.
  • The primary objective of this project is to allow ARG entities to integrate portfolio visibility into their digital experiences and financial applications.
  • The Product: The Enhance Drahim Portfolios is Powered by: ARG and it's Version: Version 1.0
