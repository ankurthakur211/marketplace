About Power Of Attorney

 

  • The Power of Attorney API product provides a digital gateway for attorney document verification and lookup services, enabling seamless integration between financial institutions and legal systems. This solution offers two core functionalities: attorney record lookup to retrieve comprehensive details about authorized representatives, and attorney information verification to validate document authenticity.
  • The system supports both Arabic and English interfaces, handles complex hierarchical attorney structures, and provides detailed verification results including attorney status, validity periods, and authorized parties. Designed for integration with banking platforms and government systems, it ensures compliance with legal standards while offering a streamlined user experience for verifying power of attorney documents.
  • The Product: Power Of Attorney is Powered by: Neotek and it's Version: Version 1.0

Description

The E-Wakalah API Suite enables secure, real-time access to the digital Power of Attorney (PoA) registry maintained by the Ministry of Justice (MOJ) in Saudi Arabia. Integrated via neotek, this API allows verified entities to retrieve, verify, and monitor active powers of attorney issued electronically, ensuring legal compliance and automation of authorization workflows across sectors.

The E-Wakalah API Suite enables
Banks & Fintechs: Validate legal authority for account access or financial decision-making.
Real Estate & Notary Services: Confirm delegation status before executing a deed.
Government & Municipal Platforms: Automate eligibility checks for authorized representatives.
Legal & Court Systems: Retrieve certified PoA details in judicial proceedings.

Use Cases

Key Features

 

 

  • Attorney Lookup

    This API aims to provide a list of names and definitions associated with specific IDs that appear in the following API so that mapping between the IDs and their associated names, such as attorney types.

  • Attorney Verification

    This API validates attorney document authenticity, verifying status, validity periods, principals/agents, and supporting bilingual (Arabic/English) data.

 

Inputs / Outputs Samples

 

  • Attorney Lookup
    Inputs: Authorization, Attorney Number, Principal ID, Agent ID
    Outputs: Control Label, Attorney Type Name
  • Attorney Verification
    Inputs: Authorization, Attorney Number, Principal ID, Agent ID
    Outputs:Status, Type of Attorney, Allowed To Act On Behalf, Principals, Agents, Agents Behavior

 

 

Key Terms

 

 

  • Type of Attorney – The type of attorney refers to the specific kind of power of attorney document, which determines the extent and duration of the agent's authority


  • Allowed To Act On Behalf – List of individuals allowed to act on behalf of the attorney


  • Status – The company is existing or has ceased its activity


  • Principals – The individuals who grant the authority to another person to act on their behalf. They are the creators of the power of attorney document and retain the power to revoke it as long as they are legally competent


  • Agents – The individuals who receive the authority from the principal to act on their behalf. They have a fiduciary duty to act in the principal's best interest.


  • Agents Behavior – Is the right of disposal for the agents jointly or individually
