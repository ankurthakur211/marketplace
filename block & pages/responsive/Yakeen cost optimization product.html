About Yakeen cost optimization

 

  • ARG’s Centralized Cached Services for Yakeen have been designed to optimize efficiency and reduce the costs of consuming Yakeen services and time. These services leverage pre-cached data already stored by the bank. When a subsidiary calls the cached service, it receives the relevant data along with the timestamp of the last caching.
  • If the provided caching date is acceptable to the subsidiary, they can proceed without calling the original source. Otherwise, the subsidiary can directly call the source systems Yakeen, to retrieve the most up-to-date information.
  • The Product: Yakeen cost optimization is Powered by: ARB and it's Version: Version 1.0
