Account Finder

Account Finder APIS

Account Finder is a service offered by ARB (an ARG Subsidiary referred to as the provider). This product allows users to view all their bank accounts across various participating banks. By displaying a comprehensive list of banks, it verifies the status of each account, identifying whether it is active or inactive. Once verified, the service searches for the user's accounts across these banks and presents a consolidated view, ensuring seamless account discovery and management.
