About Medical SME (Health Insurance)

 

  • Health Insurance as a part of the ART API Marketplace project provides financial protection and access to medical care, helping individuals and families manage healthcare costs and maintain their well-being. It's essential to choose a plan that meets your specific healthcare needs and budget.
  • The main goal is to enable ART to avail applying for Health Insurance SME policies through API Marketplace.
  • This product allows authorized users to apply for Takaful health insurance for the owners/employees and their respective dependents
  • The Product: Medical SME (Health Insurance) is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0
