About Health SME

 

  • Health SME Insurance is a specialized health insurance product provided by ART (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider) tailored to meet the needs of SME businesses. Its purpose is to offer affordable and comprehensive health coverage for employees and their relatives/dependents within SME organizations (including freelancer as long they have CR number).
  • The purpose of this project is to allow all ARG subsidiaries (referred as consumers) to offer the Health SME service to their customers through subsidiary digital channels.
  • The end customer of the service are SME companies(referred as customer) which need to issue health insurance to their employees and their dependents by utilizing ART functionalities.
  • The Product: Health SME is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0

Expose APIs as Self-Service Products

 

Get company sponsorship details

Aggregator will post a request to IC web API to this method to get CCHI (Council of Health Insurance) Minimum Count. IC will check the request and provide response (CCHI minimum count details) to Aggregator. Read more

 

Create Quote

Aggregator will post a request to ART web API to this method to get quotes. IC will check the request and provide response (quote details) to Aggregator. Read more

 

Verify Quote

Aggregator will post a request to ART web API to this method to ask ART to verify quote as sent in the quote and to check CCHI requirement for members minimum count. Read more

 

Create Policy

Aggregator uses this method to notify ART when payment acknowledgement is received for IC to issue the policy. Aggregator system will use this method to Notify and pass additional parameters to insurance company. Read more

 

Download Document

Aggregator uses this method to to download the policy document. Read more

 

Policy Callback
