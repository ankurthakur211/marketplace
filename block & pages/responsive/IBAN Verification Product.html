About IBAN Verification

 

  • The IBAN Verification serves as a robust tool empowering businesses and organizations to effortlessly obtain IBAN Verification Details by cross-referencing the bank account number with the Point of Identification (POI) number, typically the national ID.
  • It streamlines and automates the validation process, ensuring that the provided bank account is not only active but also valid. By leveraging the Saudi Payment Network, this system confirms crucial information, enhancing security measures and facilitating a seamless verification experience for users.
  • The IBAN Verification Callback feature is a designed to retrieve IBAN Verification Data from essential sources associated with a specific request. This data is validated against Saudi Payment Network, and subsequently pushed to the user.
  • The Product: IBAN Verification is Powered by: Neotek and it's Version: Version 1.0
