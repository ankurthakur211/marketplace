Emkan Calculator

Emkan Calculator is publishing their services in our API marketplace portal to be consumed by anyone of al-Rajhi group company.With Emkan Calculator, customers gain the ability to accurately assess their loan eligibility across a spectrum of products, including Top-up and Microfinance, Refinance, Retail Finance, Credit Cards, Auto Lease, and Auto Lease 50/50 and experience personalized financial insights as our platform generates initial loan offers based on specific product types entered by the user.Empower your financial journey with Emkan Calculator's intuitive tools, now available for consumption by all al-Rajhi group through the API Marketplace.

Emkan Calculator (Calculates loans for products)

Endpoint: /calculator/calculate-loans

This path is used to calculate initial loan offer for each products based on productType user input.
