About Show All Insurance Policies

 

  • Load All Policies is a product offered by ART (ARG Subsidiary referred to as the provider). It allows customers to retrieve their Takaful policies based on their national ID and policy type.
  • The objective of this project is to provide a comprehensive listing of all Takaful non-life policies created for the customer across various products. This enables easy access to policy details, improving transparency and user convenience.
  • The end customer of the service are the users of ARB App (referred as customer) which need to fetch their all policies by utilizing ART functionalities.
  • The Product: Show All Insurance Policies is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0
