About Motor Insurance

 

  • Motor insurance is a critical safeguard for vehicle owners, providing protection, legal compliance, and financial support during unforeseen situations..
  • Motor insurance is a mandatory and essential financial product that protects vehicle owners, their vehicles, and third parties from potential losses or damages caused by road accidents, theft, natural disasters, and other unforeseen events. It provides coverage for cars, motorcycles, trucks, and other types of motor vehicles, ensuring compliance with legal requirements and offering financial security.
  • The purpose of this project is to allow all ARG subsidiaries(referred as consumer) to offer the Motor insurance service to their customers through the subsidiary digital channels.
  • The Product: Motor Insurance is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0
    • Contact Us

 

 

Description

API consumers can get a quote and purchase car insurance products offered by Al Rajhi Takaful, which target individuals.

Al Rajhi Takaful offers three insurance products for individuals:

TPL

Smart

Comprehensive

These products differ in the benefits they offer.

Use Cases

Key Features

 

 

  • List Motor Products

    This API is used to get the LOVs for the products available from ART with the basic coverage and the allowed coverages (Addons) for that product.

  • Create Quotation

    A service that allows users or systems to generate insurance premium quotes for vehicles by providing necessary inputs like vehicle details, owner information, and policy preferences

  • Add a new Combine Create Quotation

    This API is used to add a new combined create Quotation; By calling it without insurance type id, you will receive the output of TPL and Comprehensive. When calling it with an insurance type id, you will receive the output of that type ID.

  • Create Draft Policy

    A service where preliminary version of an insurance policy document created before the final issuance. It serves as a reference or proposal that outlines the terms, conditions, coverage, and premiums associated with the insurance agreement. A draft policy allows for review, validation, and potential adjustments before it becomes a binding document.

  • Purchase Notification

    Consumer uses this method to Notify ART with a confirmation of policy purchase status (Success Payment or Cancel Policy).

  • List all policies

    This API is used to list all policies .

  • Policy Renewal

    This API is used to Policy Renewal .

 

Inputs / Outputs Samples

 

  • List Motor Products
    Inputs: Customer Type Id
    Outputs: List of Products with their Coverages
  • Create Quotation
    Inputs: Car Owner Details, Drivers Details, Vehicle Information
    Outputs: - List of Premiums (Prices) according to deductibles
    - List of Coverages with its premium
    - min liability & max liability
  • Add a new Combine Create Quotation
    Inputs: Car Owner Details, Chosen Product, Drivers Details, Vehicle Information, insurance type ID: 1-TPL 2- Comprehensive
    Outputs: - Prices according to deductibles
    - List of Coverages with its premium
    - min liability & max liability
  • Create Draft Policy
    Inputs: Request Reference Number, Quotation Reference Number, Deductible Reference Number, Policy Premium Coverages
    Outputs: Policy Reference Number, Policy Details
  • Purchase Notification
    Inputs: Policy Number
    Outputs: Policy Status
  • List all policies
    Inputs: POI Number
    Outputs: Policy Details
  • Policy Renewal
    Inputs: POI Number, Old Policy Id, Old Policy Number
    Outputs: Quotation Details, Policy Details

 

 

Key Terms

 

 

  • Customer Type Id- 1 for Individual and 2 for SMEs

  • TPL– Third-Party Liability


  • Deductibles – Amount of money the customer is required to pay for a covered claim before insurance company starts to pay. It is a form of cost-sharing that is a standard feature of most motor insurance policies.


  • Max liability – Maximum amount in Saudi Riyal that Insurance policy will cover against third-party liability claims.


  • Min liability – Minimum amount in Saudi Riyal that Insurance policy will cover against third-party liability claims.
