About Motor Insurance SME

 

  • Motor Insurance SME provides coverage for businesses owning commercial vehicles, fleets, or transport units. It protects against accidents, theft, natural disasters, and third-party liabilities. Policies can be customized to cover multiple vehicles, drivers, and business-specific risks. This insurance ensures legal compliance and reduces financial exposure for companies.
  • The purpose of this project is to allow all ARG subsidiaries(referred as consumer) to offer the Motor insurance SME service to their customers through the subsidiary digital channels.
  • The Product: Motor Insurance SME is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0
    • Contact Us

 

 

Description

API consumers can get a quote and purchase car insurance products offered by Al Rajhi Takaful, which target SMEs.

Al Rajhi Takaful offers two insurance products for SMEs:

TPL

Comprehensive

These products differ in the benefits they offer.

Use Cases

Key Features

 

 

  • Create Quote SME

    This API allows the consumer to receive a price quote from Al Rajhi Takaful. The quote is displayed to the customer (SME) after the consumer provides vehicle data and selects the appropriate insurance product for each car.

  • List Motor Products

    This API provides the consumer with a list of available motor insurance products and the applicable add-ons available for SMEs.

  • Purchase Notification

    This API is used after a successful payment to confirm the creation of the insurance policy. It ensures the transaction is recorded and the policy becomes active.

 

Inputs / Outputs Samples

 

  • List Motor Products
    Inputs: Customer Type: SME
    Outputs: Product ID, Name, and applicable Add-Ons
  • Create Quote SME
    Inputs: Entity Info, Car Info, and Insurance Type ID
    Outputs: Quote details
  • Purchase Notification
    Inputs: Policy Number
    Outputs: Policy Status

 

 

Key Terms

 

 

  • SME – Small and Medium-sized Enterprise

  • TPL – Third-Party Liability


  • Applicable Add-Ons – Roadside Assistance, Geographical Extension Coverage, …etc


  • Insurance Type ID – 1 = TPL, 2 = Comprehensive


  • Quote details – Policy taxable amount, Policy amount, …etc
