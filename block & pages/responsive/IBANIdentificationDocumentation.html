IBAN Identification APIs

The IBAN Identification serves as a robust tool empowering businesses and organizations to effortlessly obtain IBAN Details which allows them to verify their Beneficiary’s IBAN before making a transfer.

It streamlines and automates the validation process, ensuring that the provided bank account is not only active but also valid. By leveraging the Saudi Payment Network, this system confirms crucial information, enhancing security measures and facilitating a seamless verification experience for users.

The IBAN Identification Callback feature is a designed to retrieve IBAN Identification Data from essential sources associated with a specific request. This data is validated against Saudi Payment Network, and subsequently pushed to the user.
