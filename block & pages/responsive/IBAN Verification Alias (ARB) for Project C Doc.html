Alias Inquiry

Alias Inquiry

Alias Inquiry provides a secure and scalable solution that enables users to retrieve their linked account details using familiar identifiers such as mobile numbers, email addresses, national IDs, or account numbers. Built on the infrastructure of Saudi Payments and Al Rajhi Bank, this service ensures interoperability and efficiency across financial transactions.
