ARG Loyalty API Documentation

ARG Loyalty APIs

 

Marketplace ARG Loyalty provides APIs to Inquire and redeem loyalty points. Loyalty programs can help increase customer loyalty by rewarding customers for their interactions with the retailer's brand. Retailers can create different earning and redemption rules by tiers to differentiate the rewards for customers in different tiers.Customer will not re-fund the cash till buying in trades stock at least one time with the cash of the redeemed points. Customer’s Portfolio must be reflected with the redemption Cash and the Customer could see his credit Cash from all channels which can access his Portfolio from. The criteria of redemption method will be (280 points = 1 SAR).
