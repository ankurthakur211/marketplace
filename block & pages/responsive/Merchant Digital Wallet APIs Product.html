Merchant Digital Wallet

 

Merchant Digital Wallet is a specialized insurance product provided by Neoleap (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider) tailored to meet the needs of Neoleap customers The purpose of this project is to streamline the merchant onboarding process by determining if a merchant already exists and, if not, facilitating their registration in an efficient and secure manner. This initiative enables ARB eBusiness customers to create a URPay wallet account directly through their ARB eBusiness mobile app or portal, offering a seamless and integrated experience.
