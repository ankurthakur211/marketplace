About Merchant Digital Wallet

 

  • The API Marketplace empowers consumers to effortlessly apply for Merchant Digital Wallet through ARB, streamlining the application process for create a URPay wallet account directly through their ARB eBusiness mobile app or portal.offering a seamless and integrated experience. This initiative enables ARB eBusiness customers to create a URPay wallet account directly through their ARB eBusiness mobile app or portal, offering a seamless and integrated experience.
  • The purpose of Merchant digital wallet :
    • Provide an easy, fast, and secure registration process for ARB customers.
    • Enable ARB merchants to seamlessly access the URPay Wallet.
    • Offer enhanced usability for financial transactions
  • The Product: Merchant Digital Wallet is Powered by: Neoleap and it's Version: Version 1.0
