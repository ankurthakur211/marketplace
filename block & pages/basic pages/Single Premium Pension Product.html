About Single Premium Pension

 

  • The high-level document illustrates the overall architectural view and the solution approach to fulfill the requirements of Single Premium Pension and deliver the intended outcomes and product with the best quality that satisfies the bank customers and to achieve the bank objectives.
  • The main goal for this project is to Allowing ARB Mobile App customers to apply for Single Premium Pension Product through retail application until product complete sale.
  • A single premium policy is one where the policyholder pays the entire premium amount upfront at the start of the policy term. This is in contrast to traditional insurance policies where the premium is paid periodically over the term of the policy.
  • The Product: Single Premium Pension is Powered by: ARB and it's Version: Version 1.0
