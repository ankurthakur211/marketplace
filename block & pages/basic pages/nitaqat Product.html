About Nitaqat

 

  • Nitaqat service offered by Neotek facilitates providing accurate, real-time establishment insights.

Inputs / Outputs Samples

 

  • Get Basic Establishment Details
    Inputs: CR Number,700 Number
    Outputs: Company Size,Establishment Economic Activity,Establishment Status
  • Get Advanced Establishment Details
    Inputs: CR Number,700 Number
    Outputs:the same basic plus: Saudization Percentage,Count Of Saudis,Count Of Foreigners
  • Purchase Notification
    Inputs: CR Number,700 Number
    Outputs:the same advanced plus: Count Of Violations,Sum Of Unpaid Violations,Nitaq Color

 

 

Key Terms

 

 

  • CR Number – Commercial Registration


  • 700 Number – Standard identification number issued by the Ministry of Commerce


  • Status – The company is existing or has ceased its activity


  • ISIC Activity – International Standard Industrial Classification of All Economic Activities


  • Size – A company’s size is determined by its total number of employees. Saudization quotas for the number of Saudis to employ vary depending on the size of a company and, therefore, impact its Nitaqat classification or ranking. 


  • Saudization Percentage – The proportion of Saudi nationals employed within a company or sector compared to the total workforce


  • Nitaq Color – refers to a company's classification based on its Saudization rate, which is the proportion of Saudi nationals it employs. These color-coded classifications (Platinum, High Green, Medium Green, Low Green, and Red), for example Platinum Represents the highest level of Saudization compliance, offering the most benefits and expedited services


  • Saudization Compliance – Saudization compliance refers to the adherence of companies operating in Saudi Arabia to the government's policy of employing a certain percentage of Saudi nationals in their workforce
