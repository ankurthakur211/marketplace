ARG Loyalty

 

Marketplace ARG Loyalty provides APIs to Inquire and redeem loyalty points. Loyalty programs can help increase customer loyalty by rewarding customers for their interactions with the retailer's brand. Those benefits may be in the form of discounts, rebates, free products, or other promotion.
