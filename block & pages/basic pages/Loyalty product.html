About ARG Loyalty

Marketplace ARG Loyalty provides APIs to Inquire and redeem loyalty points. Loyalty programs can help increase customer loyalty by rewarding customers for their interactions with the retailer's brand. Retailers can create different earning and redemption rules by tiers to differentiate the rewards for customers in different tiers.
