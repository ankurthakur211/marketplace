About Cyber Insurance

 

  • Cyber Security Insurance is a product provided by ART (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider) This product provides coverage to protect companies from financial losses and liabilities resulting from cyber-attacks and data breaches.
  • The purpose of this project is to allow ARG subsidiaries (referred as consumers) to offer cyber security insurance service to their customers through subsidiary digital channels.
  • The end customers of the service are corporate clients (referred to as customers) who are eligible to request cyber security insurance.
  • The Product: Cyber Insurance is Powered by: ART and it's Version: Version 1.0
