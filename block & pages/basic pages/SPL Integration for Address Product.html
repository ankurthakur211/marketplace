SPL Integration for Address

SPL Integration for Address is a specialized insurance product provided by Neotek (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider) tailored is to enable all ARG subsidiaries (referred to as "consumers") to provide national address information to their customers through their respective digital channels. It supports two primary user types: establishments and individuals, ensuring the most reliable and up-to-date address details are obtained directly from the authoritative source, Saudi Post Logistics.
