Terminologies (Business Definitions)

 

  • Mutual Fund

    An investment product where multiple parties contribute capital into a professionally managed pool, providing diversified asset exposure.

  • Subscription

    The process of committing funds to acquire a proportional share in the investment pool.

  • Redemption

    The act of withdrawing part or all of the invested share in exchange for its current value.

  • Portfolio Management

    The oversight and reporting of invested holdings, including performance tracking and valuation updates.

  • Portfolio Holdings

    The detailed record of all investment units owned by a customer, including fund names, quantities, acquisition dates, and related attributes, representing their current positions in the investment pool.

  • Portfolio Performance

    The measurement and reporting of how a customer's holdings have changed in value over time, including gains, losses, dividends, and overall return, enabling informed investment decisions.

 

 

Key Features

 

 

  • Subscription Capabilities

    - Access to a complete and up-to-date catalogue of available funds with relevant attributes.
    - Automated eligibility and suitability checks before allowing an investment.
    - Flexible investment amounts based on the fund's minimum and maximum thresholds.
    - Immediate confirmation of successful subscriptions with unique transaction IDs.

  • Redemption Capabilities

    - Ability to request partial or full withdrawal from owned funds.
    - Handling of settlement timelines according to the fund's operational rules.
    - Status tracking from request initiation to completion.

  • Portfolio Management

    - Consolidated view of all customer holdings across multiple funds.
    - Real-time updates of fund quantities, valuations, and asset composition.
    - Exportable reports for compliance and record-keeping.

  • Reporting & Notifications

    - Transaction summaries for subscriptions and redemptions.
    - Periodic performance statements for each customer portfolio.
    - Alerts for key events such as NAV changes, dividend distributions, or fund suspensions.

 

Inputs / Outputs Samples

 

  • Inputs (Business Perspective):
    - Customer identification details.
    - Fund selection and subscription/redemption details.
    - Payment/wallet information.
    - Risk assessment questionnaire responses.

  • Outputs (Business Perspective):
    - Fund details and pricing data.
    - Subscription/redemption confirmation and status.
    - Portfolio summaries and performance reports.
    - Notifications on transactions and dividends.
