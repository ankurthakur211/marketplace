About Yakeen Integration

 

  • Yakeen Integration is a product provided by ARB (ARG Subsidiary referred as provider). This product allows you to retrieve personal information and customer ID expiration details from Yakeen.
  • The purpose of this project is to allow ARG subsidiaries (referred as consumers) to offer Yakeen integration service to their customers through subsidiary digital channels.
  • The end customers of the service are any customer (referred to as customers) who are eligible to inquire about person information and customer ID expiration.
  • The Product: Yakeen Integration is Powered by: ARB and it's Version: Version 1.0
