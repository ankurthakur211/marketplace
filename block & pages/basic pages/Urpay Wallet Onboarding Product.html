About Urpay Wallet Onboarding

 

  • The purpose of this project is to allow ARG subsidiaries (referred as consumers) to offer an urpay wallet service to their customers through subsidiary digital channels
  • The end customers of the service are any customers (referred to as customers) who can create accounts on Urpay.
