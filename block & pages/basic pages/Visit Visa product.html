About Visit Visa Extension

 

  • API Marketplace is offering all al-Rajhi group to share their most consumed APIs in its portal to make it easy to call external entities and call each other with low-rate API call.
  • This API service facilitates the validation of customer visa details, retrieval of visit visa duration, fetching medical declaration questions, obtaining policy price, and creating a Visit Visa Extension policy.
  • The main goal for this project is to enable ART to avail applying for visitor visa extension through API Marketplace.
  • The visitor visa extension works on those who are in the Kingdom only and wish to extend their visit 7 days or less before its end, provided that the visitor is eligible to travel and that there are no traffic rules for him.
