About Life Protection & Saving

 

  • API Marketplace is offering all al-Rajhi group to share their most consumed APIs in its portal to make it easy to call external entities and call each other with low-rate API call.
  • Protect and saving policies typically refer to various measures, strategies, or agreements designed to safeguard assets, resources, or individuals' interests, often within the context of finance, insurance, or risk management. These policies aim to mitigate risks, provide financial security, and promote responsible stewardship of resources.
  • The main goal for this project is to enable ART to avail applying for protection and saving policies through API Marketplace.
  • The Protection and Savings policies operate on the principle of saving a specific amount of money placed in a Takaful Fund on a monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual basis. The subscriber's need is then covered from this fund according to the type and details of the protection and savings policies.
