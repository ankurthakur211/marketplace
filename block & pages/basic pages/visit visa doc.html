Visit Visa Extension APIs

Visit Visa Extension service facilitates the validation of customer visa details, retrieval of visit visa duration, fetching medical declaration questions, obtaining policy price, and creating a Visit Visa Extension policy. The main goal for this project is to enable ART to avail applying for visitor visa extension through API Marketplace. The visitor visa extension works on those who are in the Kingdom only and wish to extend their visit 7 days or less before its end, provided that the visitor is eligible to travel and that there are no traffic rules for him.
